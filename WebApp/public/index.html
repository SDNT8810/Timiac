MOTOR DRIVERS · LIVE WHEN IDLEConnecting

Automatic checks every 2 seconds while idle. Each board is checked independently; no homing or control is needed. Green = checks passed · Red = no response · Amber = settings mismatch · Gray = no current check.

Disconnect board power and USB before changing a driver. After power returns, results refresh automatically. No response can mean a damaged driver, socket, jumper or power problem; a green result does not test the motor output.
